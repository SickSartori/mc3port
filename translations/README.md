# Translations

## Português (Brasil): `pt-br.mc3patch`

Tradução e romhack: **refresh**, version 3 of 18 August 2026
([Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/midnightclub3dubeditionremix/mods/46)), made for the PS2 game (SLUS-21355) and used in mc3port with his permission.

mc3port carries it to the Xbox game: the same texts (the string tables, the subtitles, the loading tips), his fonts
with the letters he drew (Ã, Õ, Â, Ê…), the menus' and loading screens' Flash files with his glyphs, and the
navigation system's title. About 240 strings that the Xbox words differently (its buttons, its saves, Xbox Live) are
mc3port's, written in his style. `pt-br.mc3patch` holds, for each of the 745 files it changes, the difference from the
disc's file (VCDIFF) and the SHA-1s of both, so it contains no file of the game. mc3port makes the translated files from
the player's own disc the first time Português (Brasil) is chosen (`src/pc/translation.cpp`); the European and the USA
discs both work. How it was made: sick-xport, `projects/mc3port/MODS-VALUTAZIONE.md`.

Added to mc3port at the suggestion of [NAKADANI's video](https://youtu.be/i5IWJ2krkeo).
